How the cycle works

1. The 5-hour and 2-hour timers start together.

2. When 2 hours end, both pause and the 20-minute timer starts.

3. After 20 minutes, the 2-hour timer resets and both focus timers resume.

4. Whenever the 5-hour timer reaches zero, it resets to 5 hours and continues counting during the focus phase.

5. Pause stops the entire system. Start resumes it. Reset All restores the initial state.
